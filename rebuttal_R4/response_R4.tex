I thank the Editor for accepting the manuscript and for the precise
indication of what remained to be done. The changes made in this round are
shown in blue in the marked copy. Because long sentences ran through most of the
text, the revision touched most paragraphs, and much of the marked copy is
therefore blue; no scientific content, figure, table or reference has changed.

\R{English language and sentence structure.}
\begin{rquote}
``English language needs editing and improvement (around 85\% ready) to meet
publication standards; numerous instances of unclear sentence structures.
[\dots] ideas need to be broken up into separate sentences.''
\end{rquote}
The whole text, from the abstract to the conclusions, has been revised to
break long sentences into separate ones, each carrying a single idea. Clauses
joined by semicolons, chains of subordinate clauses and long parenthetical
remarks have been rewritten as sentences of their own. The example given has
become four sentences:
\begin{quote}
The within-cluster dispersion that these criteria minimise is a
variance-based quantity. A reference matched to the data's spatial covariance
structure, its variogram, therefore reproduces the same dispersion whether or
not classes are present. This happens because the between-class contrast is
itself part of the covariance that the reference is matched to. The comparison
then has nothing left to detect.
\end{quote}
The effect of the revision on sentence structure across the main text is as
follows.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
 & Previous version & Revised version\\
\midrule

\end{center}

Two sentences remain above 35 words, deliberately. One lists the preprocessing
settings of a robustness check in Section~5.3 and reads more clearly as a single
list. The other, in Section~6, ends with a clause that qualifies only the
stopping-rule use of the method; splitting it would extend that qualification to
every use.
The abstract was revised in the same
way, and the abstract in the submission metadata has been updated to match it.
